# Robert's Rules in Robbie

What Robbie supports of Robert's Rules of Order Newly Revised (12th edition), as of 2026-10-08. Robbie offers the motions an HOA meeting uses and makes each one work end to end: in the meeting's state, on the question card, in the chair's toolbar, on phones, on the display and in the minutes. The rest are not in Robbie: no screen offers them and the server refuses them as unknown motions; the table below says what to do instead. A live meeting or record saved before they were removed still reads (`RETIRED_MOTIONS`, `shared/constants/motions.ts`). The decisions and their reasons: `docs/design/2026-10-08-meeting-rules-design.md`; for elections, thresholds and voice votes, `docs/design/2026-10-08-elections-and-thresholds-design.md`; for board meetings and the meeting notice, `docs/design/2026-10-09-board-meetings-and-notice-design.md`.

## Motions

| Motion                                                                | Supported    | What adoption does, and the gaps                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Main motion                                                           | Yes          | One at a time, only once the agenda is adopted. The chair can also put a question on an agenda item, with no mover.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| Amend the bylaws                                                      | Yes          | The vote the organization's bylaws require (Settings, Bylaw amendments): two thirds (the default) or a majority, of the votes cast or of all the voting members, stamped on the motion when it is moved. Counted, never declared by voice. Its words are the bylaw text and can't be amended in the meeting (withdraw and move again). The text adopted becomes a new version of the bylaws. |
| Adopt or amend the agenda                                             | Yes          | After an objection to adopting the agenda without one. Minuted.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Amend, amend the amendment                                            | Yes          | Insert, strike, strike and insert, or replace the whole text, of a main motion; a secondary amendment changes the words the primary inserts. Adoption rewrites the words beneath, which are then put, stamped and minuted (with the words as first moved). Amending other motions (a recess time, a referral) isn't offered.                                                                 |
| Close debate (previous question)                                      | Yes          | Two thirds. Ends debate on the immediately pending question only.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| Postpone                                                              | Yes          | To the next meeting (listed in the minutes for its agenda) or to later in the meeting (the chair takes it up again; not taken up, it is unfinished business at the adjournment). Takes the main motion and its pending amendments.                                                                                                                                                           |
| Postpone indefinitely                                                 | Yes          | Kills the main motion for the meeting.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Refer                                                                 | Yes          | To a committee or the board, with any pending amendments.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| Recess                                                                | Yes          | Optional time to resume; the chair resumes the meeting; pending business resumes.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| Adjourn                                                               | Yes          | Carries even with business pending, which is recorded as unfinished; the chair then declares the meeting adjourned. With nothing pending the chair adjourns without a motion. Not reconsiderable.                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| Withdraw                                                              | Yes          | At once while awaiting a second; once stated, the mover asks and the request is granted without objection or by a vote. The chair records it for a mover in the room.                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| Point of order                                                        | Yes          | In order during a vote and while a motion awaits a second; holds everything until the chair rules: well taken, well taken with the motion ruled out of order (removed with a record), or not well taken.                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| Appeal                                                                | Yes          | At once after a ruling; yes sustains the chair, a tie sustains; reversing an out of order ruling puts the motion back.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Request for information, parliamentary inquiry, question of privilege | As questions | Asked of the chair and answered by the chair; not motions and not minuted.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| Division of the assembly                                              | Yes          | While a voice vote is open, or right after the chair declares its result, until other business comes up (RONR 29:7; changing the way of voting is other business): the decision is undone and the vote counted instead, on devices and in the room, with hands raised since kept.                                                                                                            |
| Unanimous consent                                                     | Yes          | The chair asks on the pending question; one objection (from a phone, or recorded from the floor) means a vote; the request ends when a vote opens, a motion is made or the question changes. Not on an appeal.                                                                                                                                                                               |
| Lay on the table, take from the table                                 | No           | Postpone to later in the meeting instead.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| Reconsider, rescind, amend something previously adopted               | No           | A decision stands until a later meeting changes it.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| Suspend the rules                                                     | No           |                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Divide the question                                                   | No           | Amend the motion, or move its parts separately.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Objection to the consideration of a question                          | No           | Vote it down, or postpone it indefinitely.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| Call for the orders of the day                                        | No           | Raise a point of order, or ask the chair.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| Fix the time to adjourn to                                            | No           | Adjourn, and schedule the next meeting.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| Limit or extend debate                                                | No           | The chair sets the speaking time; close debate ends it.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |

## Rules of the meeting

| Rule           | Supported | Notes                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| -------------- | --------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Precedence     | Yes       | One shared rule for the screens and the server: one main motion at a time, one primary and one secondary amendment, subsidiary motions outrank the question they apply to, adjourn outranks recess, an election holds the floor.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| Seconds        | Yes       | Recorded from phones or from the floor; a motion not seconded dies, and the minutes say so.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| Debate         | Yes       | The mover is recognized first only while waiting to speak; members may speak on both sides; anyone may ask for the floor whenever the meeting is in session and nothing is being voted on (an open forum, questions on a report). No limit on how often a member speaks.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| Voting         | Yes       | Majority or two thirds of the votes cast (abstentions don't count, a tie fails, an appeal's tie sustains the chair), or of all the voting members for bylaw amendments when the organization says so ("Two thirds of all 142 voting members: 95 votes needed", with a warning when fewer are present: "Only 29 present: this can't pass"); device votes plus the chair's count of the room; voice, standard, roll call and secret ballot. The chair may declare a voice vote on a majority question ("The ayes have it"), minuted "by voice vote". Not supported: a threshold of the members present.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| Quorum         | Yes       | Shown on the console and the display; opening a vote without one asks the chair first, and the minutes say the action has no effect unless ratified. Adjourn and recess need no quorum.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Elections      | Yes       | One office at a time, for one seat or several on one ballot (each voter marks up to that many names); majority, plurality or two thirds of the ballots cast, devices and paper together (blank paper ballots not counted, illegal ones counted). The candidates with the vote required fill the seats; a tie for the last seat, or nobody with the vote, takes another ballot with every candidate (RONR 46:32; under a plurality, the tied run off); the winner of one seat is off the next ballot, and nobody is elected twice (a write-in or a declaration naming them is refused). A ballot with none cast doesn't close. Seats left open with nobody on the ballot go back to nominations, as the same election. The tellers' count of paper ballots is the chair's until the ballot closes. Names written in on paper are counted and minuted as write-ins; a device can vote only for a candidate. No more nominees than seats may be declared elected by acclamation (RONR 46:40), if the bylaws allow it (the chair confirms). The chair declares each winner of a closed ballot, and only them; an election is set aside only while nobody awaits a declaration.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Minutes        | Yes       | Generated from the meeting's records: each decision and how it was disposed of, points of order and rulings, the agenda's adoption, recesses, elections (one paragraph each, with every ballot's totals), unfinished business and business postponed to the next meeting. A report taken up with no motion is "Report received." Times are given for the call to order, recesses and the adjournment, not each vote.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| Board meetings | Yes       | A meeting is of the members or of the board. In a board meeting the directors (members marked as on the board) vote and count toward the quorum, a majority of them unless the organization sets a board quorum (never more than the directors); the presiding officer chairs, a secretary or admin who isn't a director keeps the console without a vote (recording what people in the room do: a motion or second by a director, an objection, a division, a nomination), and the organization's other members observe: they may ask to speak and ask the chair, and the chair decides whom to recognize, but they don't move, second, vote or raise a point of order. Movers and seconders from the floor are directors named by their accounts; nobody is counted in the room and nobody votes by proxy; each director votes once, on a device or by hand, and paper ballots are capped the same way. With no more than 12 directors the chair, a director, votes on every question and may move and second like any director (RONR 49:21); a larger board's chair votes only to decide, as at a meeting of the members. Bylaw amendments are the members' and can't be moved there; elections (a board electing its officers) work. The minutes list the directors present and absent and who else attended, and approve the board's own previous minutes. Not supported: executive session (recess, and summarize it in the minutes), committees. |
| Notice         | Courtesy  | A secretary emails the notice of a scheduled meeting (date, place, agenda, its documents and the link) to every member and everyone added who hasn't signed in, and prints it with a QR code for posting and mailing. It is a courtesy: the bylaws and state law set the official notice, and Robbie doesn't keep a record of delivery beyond how many it went to and how many failed (a notice no email reached isn't recorded as sent). An organization sends at most 3 a day, canceled meetings' notices included.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| Proxies        | No        | Paper proxies are counted by marking the holder present and counting the vote in the room.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
